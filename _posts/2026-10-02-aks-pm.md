Some news: I am now a program manager on Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS).

Containers have been part of my work for many years, and I have blogged about Kubernetes and AKS a couple of times. So in a way this feels like coming back to familiar ground, now from the product side.

This week I have been transitioning and ramping up, mostly learning about the team and the technology. For instance, I hadn't experimented with AKS Automatic yet, which is very interesting.
